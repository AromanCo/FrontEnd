## Confidentiality and evidence handling

- Stage 1 should disclose only an approved, minimal description of the inquiry. Do not request formulas, ingredient ratios, confidential methods, customer data, or sensitive documents.
- Before stage 2, have an authorized reviewer assess whether an NDA or another confidentiality agreement is appropriate, who may sign it, its scope, permitted use, retention/deletion, and whether the recipient is the actual rights holder.
- Use an approved restricted-access channel for documents. Store only a non-sensitive reference, date, document type/version, scope, custodian and verification status in this register.
- “Response received” and “Documents received” are not “Independently verified.” The latter requires a named qualified reviewer, an appropriate source/issuer check, and a recorded method/date/scope/result.
- A company registration, directory entry, certificate copy, or marketing statement alone does not establish manufacturing authorization, product safety, quality, Iran compliance, production capacity, or release readiness.
