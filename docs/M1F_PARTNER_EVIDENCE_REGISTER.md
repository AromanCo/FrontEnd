# M1-F Partner and Manufacturing Evidence Register

Status: template only. No supplier or manufacturing candidate has been researched or contacted for this register; no candidate documentation has been requested, received, or independently verified.

Use one record per organization and capability. A company may have separate records for B and C because its evidence, scope, process, capacity, and approvals may differ. Do not treat a marketing statement, referral, or document receipt as independent verification. Link each assertion to its source, date, applicability, reviewer, and verification result.

## Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not researched | No candidate/source investigation recorded. |
| Third-party claimed | A claim exists, but it has not been substantiated. Record the claimant and source. |
| Documentation requested | A specific written item has been requested; it has not been received. |
| Documentation received | A file or written response was received; authenticity, currency, scope, and adequacy are still unverified. |
| Independently verified | A named qualified reviewer checked the evidence against the stated scope and recorded method/date/result. This is not automatic product-line approval. |
| Rejected | Candidate or evidence is unsuitable for the recorded scope; retain rationale and source history. |
| Approval pending | Evidence review is complete or substantially complete, but a named required approval decision remains open. |

## Evidence handling and comparison rules

1. Preserve exact source and date. A website is evidence that a statement was published, not proof that the capability is current, applicable, authorized, or independently validated.
2. Track each document separately as requested, received, and independently verified. Record issuer, revision, validity/effective date, scope, authenticity check, reviewer, and findings.
3. Request product- or method-specific evidence. A general capability statement does not establish a particular SKU, option, configuration, batch, location, or market.
4. Record written commercial terms only with source and conditions. Do not estimate MOQ, price, lead time, capacity, or availability from informal language.
5. For C, record approved configuration/version provenance and change/withdrawal controls only if documented. A Studio concept or advisory output is not a candidate configuration.
6. Keep confidential manufacturing documents access-controlled and out of public frontend assets/APIs. Minimize customer or personal data in research records.
7. Use the separate [production decision matrix](M1F_PRODUCTION_GO_NO_GO.md) for approval. `Independently verified` in this register does not automatically advance a product line to prototype, pilot, or commercial release.

## Minimum evidence dimensions for later comparison

| Dimension | B evidence focus | C evidence focus |
|---|---|---|
| Identity and ownership | Finished product/SKU, brand authorization, product and media rights | Production method, configuration owner, option and specification version ownership |
| Manufacturing | Product-specific source, process scope, batch identity | Approved option/control system, configuration-to-production traceability |
| Quality/repeatability | Batch records, quality controls, defects and consistency evidence | Repeatability across allowed configurations/options and change control |
| Packaging/labeling | Product components, packaging supply, label responsibility | Whether the controlled offer's package/label changes with options and who controls it |
| Samples | Finished sample, written criteria, approval record | Prototype representative of a documented configuration and approved customer options |
| Capacity/terms | SKU MOQ, capacity, replenishment, written cost/lead time | Per-order or batch model, option-level capacity, MOQ, written lead time and quote inputs |
| Operational lifecycle | Storage, transport, returns, defects, discontinuation | Prohibited/withdrawn choices, outstanding quotes/orders, historical version handling |

All dimensions begin **Not verified**. There is no selected supplier or preferred candidate in this register.
