## Common qualification and evidence questions

1. What exact legal entity would contract with AROMAN, and which entity performs each manufacturing, packaging, testing, storage, and shipping activity?
2. Which facilities, geographies, product categories, and operational stages are within the proposed scope? Which are excluded or subcontracted?
3. What current manufacturing, quality, traceability, and change-control documents can you provide for the relevant scope? Who issued them, what revisions and effective dates apply, and how can their authenticity be checked independently?
4. Which qualified parties perform product-specific quality, safety, labeling, or regulatory reviews? What do they review, what records are produced, and what is explicitly outside their authority?
5. What records identify a sample and production batch, link materials/process revisions to finished goods, and support investigation of a defect or withdrawal?
6. How are deviations, complaints, recalls/withdrawals, rejected batches, and customer-impacting changes communicated? What are notification timelines and responsible contacts?
7. Which written terms govern confidentiality, AROMAN/customer information, IP, document access, subcontracting, retention, return/destruction, and termination?
8. Which claims in your response are supported only by marketing material or verbal explanation? Please identify the primary evidence needed to substantiate each claim.

## B — AROMAN Signature Collection

Answer for each proposed finished product/SKU separately. Do not combine general capabilities with product-specific evidence.

### Manufacturing model and product ownership

1. Is the proposed relationship contract manufacturing, private label, white label, distribution, or another model? Please provide the proposed legal entity and a written model description.
2. Who owns the product identity, product name, brand, design, product-specific documentation, and rights to use manufacturing records? Which rights would AROMAN receive, for what territory and duration?
3. Is finished-fragrance development included? If yes, describe the documented scope and decision points without disclosing or proposing unapproved formulas in this questionnaire.
4. Which product specifications and revisions would define an approved finished item? How are changes proposed, reviewed, approved, versioned, and communicated before production?

### Samples and product evaluation

5. What is the written sample-request and sample-approval workflow? Who prepares, identifies, ships, and signs off on each sample?
6. What sample identity, batch/source, date, storage/handling information, and supporting documents accompany a sample?
7. How would AROMAN record acceptance criteria, required qualified reviews, rejection, correction, and re-sampling? Which sample results do not establish production quality or regulatory approval?

### Finished goods and packaging

8. Which finished sizes or variants are actually available for the proposed SKU? Provide a dated, product-specific written list.
9. Who sources and owns the bottle, atomizer, cap, carton, inserts, and other packaging components? Which components are standard, custom, subcontracted, or subject to minimum quantities?
10. Who is responsible for packaging compatibility, component quality, assembly, tamper evidence if applicable, and packaging change control? Provide evidence and review owner.
11. Who prepares, reviews, approves, prints, and applies each label or consumer-facing disclosure? Which qualified local review is still required before any Iran-market use?

### Quantity, quality, capacity, and timing

12. What written MOQ applies to each exact product, size, package, and order condition? State unit, date, validity, and any component-level MOQ.
13. What quality-control and batch-traceability records are available? How are acceptance criteria and production deviations documented?
14. What evidence supports consistency across batches and repeat orders? Provide the applicable time period, product scope, and records that can be independently reviewed.
15. What capacity is reserved or realistically available, for which product/site/time period, and what written evidence supports it?
16. What are the sample, first-production, replenishment, and shipping lead times? Provide dated written estimates/quotes and identify dependencies, exclusions, and uncertainty.
17. How are out-of-stock, delayed, discontinued, defective, damaged, or nonconforming goods handled? Who authorizes replacements, returns, credit, rework, and customer communication?

### Commercial and operational terms

18. Provide written cost/quote structure, currency, quote validity, payment milestones, taxes/fees exclusions, and conditions. Do not treat this response as an approved AROMAN retail price.
19. What storage conditions, shelf-life information, handling instructions, shipment restrictions, and transport responsibilities are documented for the finished SKU? Identify the source and reviewer.
20. Which party handles delivery claims, damage, returns, customer complaints, product withdrawal, and unsold/obsolete packaging or stock?
21. What contract terms address exclusivity, territory, subcontracting, termination, continuity, ownership of product-specific work, and AROMAN branding rights?

## C — AROMAN Controlled Personalization

Answer for a specific proposed physical method and option system. Do not infer that the current Studio's note/layer model is usable as a production method.

### Method, authority, and permitted choices

1. What physical personalization method do you actually support? Describe the operational boundary and provide current written evidence. Do not provide undocumented formulas or chemical proportions as a substitute for approved specifications.
2. Do manufacturer-approved bases, accords, options, or other constrained selections currently exist for this method? If so, identify the controlled documents, revisions, owners, effective scope, and access method. If not, state that clearly.
3. Who has authority and qualifications to define, review, approve, publish, suspend, and withdraw permitted combinations and customer-facing options?
4. Which customer choices can be offered without exposing unrestricted formulation details or relying on Studio note IDs? How are option labels and descriptions reviewed for accuracy?
5. What validation prevents an unapproved, unknown, stale, withdrawn, or out-of-scope option combination from entering a production request?

### Configuration versioning and repeatability

6. How does each selectable configuration map to a controlled production specification and exact versions of its inputs/rules? Can historical configurations be reproduced and audited without silently updating old records?
7. How are requested options, approvals, manufacturer changes, effective dates, and withdrawal/revocation decisions recorded? What is the immutable history and who can change status?
8. What evidence supports repeatability for each allowed option/configuration and across production batches? What test/sample records and qualified reviewer sign-offs are available?
9. Can the system support per-customer/per-order production? If not, describe batch, campaign, or minimum production constraints with written evidence.

### Samples, capacity, pricing, and operations

10. What is the sample/prototype process for each proposed configuration? How is the sample tied to an exact configuration version, production document, and review record?
11. Who owns quality, safety, labeling, claims, and product approval review for the controlled product? Which determinations require qualified local professional or authority review?
12. What MOQ, production capacity, lead time, pricing inputs, packaging, fulfillment, and replenishment terms apply to each option/configuration? Provide current dated written evidence and its conditions.
13. How are capacity and availability checked at eligibility, quote, and order time? What causes an option to become unavailable, and how quickly does that status propagate?
14. What happens to open drafts, quotes, accepted orders, and customer support cases when a rule, option, or configuration is suspended or withdrawn?
15. Which changes require a new sample, renewed review, new configuration version, customer reconfirmation, or suspension of availability?

### Confidentiality and ownership

16. Who owns the approved configuration, permitted-combination rules, technical records, and resulting customer-facing descriptions? What rights can AROMAN use, maintain, and audit?
17. How are confidential formula/specification documents protected, access-controlled, retained, and securely returned/deleted at contract end?
18. Which information can be shared with frontend/backend systems as non-confidential option IDs and descriptions, and which must remain in restricted partner/manufacturer records?
19. How are supplier substitutions, manufacturing-site changes, and partner termination handled without representing an old configuration as currently approved?

## Iran-specific discovery questions (no compliance conclusion)

These are questions for official-source research and qualified local advice, not assertions that a particular rule applies.

1. How should the proposed product and each line (B and C) be classified for the intended activities and sales model? Which current official source or authority confirms the classification?
2. Which authorities and current primary documents govern the proposed manufacturing, product, import/local sourcing, packaging, labeling, and sale scope?
3. Which qualified local professionals should review product safety, claims, labels, business/entity, trademark, tax/invoicing, consumer policies, privacy, payment/settlement, and logistics?
4. What evidence is required for manufacturing-site/product approval, quality documents, product identity, ingredient restrictions, consumer disclosures, and change/withdrawal handling, if applicable to the final model?
5. Which components/materials are local or imported, and what current written evidence addresses sourcing continuity, permissions, customs/logistics, and delivery constraints?
6. Which legal entity can contract, hold relevant rights, invoice, receive eligible payments, handle complaints, and support the proposed product line? What remains unresolved pending professional review?
