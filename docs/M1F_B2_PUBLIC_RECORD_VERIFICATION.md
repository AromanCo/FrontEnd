Exact-name web searches also did not return an attributable official record for the Iranian candidates. A third-party 2021 job notice also advertised production roles at an Ivanaki factory for «شرکت عطر اتحاد پایتخت». This supports a historical lead for follow-up, not present operation or legal identity.
- No independently authenticated certificate, perfume-specific license, current operational capacity, sample, site audit, third-party contract manufacturing, or controlled-personalization system was established for any candidate.

The B.1 marketing-claim record remains intact in [the evidence register](M1F_PARTNER_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md). This report adds an independent-source layer; it does not rewrite B.1 history.

## Method and source selection

Research was limited to pages and documents publicly accessible on 2026-10-23. Priority was given to official government or institutional sources where accessible, then to public association/event records and other attributable independent publications. Candidate websites were used only to identify claims and compare them with external records.

Source classes used here:

1. **Official public system** — government registry/agency or its published record. A portal’s description of its function is not a candidate-specific lookup result.
2. **Institutional directory or association publication** — an industrial-zone directory, chamber/association list, or event exhibitor list. It corroborates only what that publication lists, at the stated date and scope.
3. **Secondary public dataset** — an aggregator or index that attributes its data to a public/official source. It is not a substitute for the originating record.
4. **Company-published claim** — evidence that the organization published the claim. It is not independent corroboration.

For Iran, the commercial secondary source [InfoProff’s Iran register overview](https://www.infoproff.com/en/open-data/iran/183/business-register-in-iran) identifies `sherkat.ssaa.ir`, `irsherkat.ssaa.ir`, `ilenc.ssaa.ir` and `rrk.ir` as company-register, legal-person ID and Gazette channels, and describes their claimed scopes. This source map is not itself an Iranian authority publication; this review did not independently establish each endpoint’s provenance from an accessible SSAA/authority page. Direct requests to the listed endpoints timed out in the web research environment, and exact-name searches restricted to them returned no indexed result. No access controls were bypassed and no accounts were created. Thus both candidate searches and the authority-source check remain limited.

For Turkey, the [Ministry of Trade MERSİS page](https://mersis.ticaret.gov.tr/) was accessible and describes MERSİS as a centralized system for company registration and commercial registry records. Its landing page links the [Türkiye Ticaret Sicili Gazetesi](https://www.ticaretsicil.gov.tr/). No candidate-specific MERSİS/Gazette lookup result was obtained. The Gazette page did not load in this review. No registration/login was attempted.

**No negative inference:** “not found,” timeout, blocked, stale, or inaccessible means the evidence was not established in this review. It does not establish nonexistence, nonregistration, invalidity, or nonoperation.

## Separate product-line evidence gaps
