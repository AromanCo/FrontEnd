# M1-F — Signature Collection B Product Brief V1

- **Status:** Internal creative direction — working brief.
- **Product line:** B — AROMAN Signature Collection.
- **Intended market:** Iran, subject to future qualified legal, regulatory and operational review.
- **Manufacturing status:** No qualified partner, formula, sample, production scope or release approval has been established.
- **Outreach permission:** Not authorized.

## 1. Purpose and status

This brief records the product owner's preferences and proposed creative direction for future AROMAN-branded finished fragrances. It is an internal development brief, not a supplier specification, formula, claim approval, purchase commitment, or authorization to contact a supplier.

It follows the existing separation between a creative concept, optional advisory guidance, and an approved production configuration in [M1-E.2 Product Direction](M1E2_PRODUCT_DIRECTION.md). The independent B/C readiness gates in [M1-F Production Go/No-Go](M1F_PRODUCTION_GO_NO_GO.md) remain unchanged and **Not verified**.

## 2. Product-owner preferences and proposed hypotheses

### Confirmed product-owner preferences

- AROMAN prefers original, exclusive-to-brand fragrances developed specifically for AROMAN, subject to feasibility and future agreements establishing the necessary rights.
- The primary intended audience is modern fragrance enthusiasts in Iran who seek distinctive original scents and personal expression.
- Supplier outreach is not authorized.

These are directions and preferences. They do not establish customer demand, manufacturing feasibility, ownership or exclusivity rights, legal status, or commercial readiness.

### Proposed positioning hypotheses

AROMAN may be presented as a contemporary, premium-leaning, design-led fragrance brand. The working English positioning is:

> Fragrance for people who want to smell like themselves.

Proposed Persian communication:

> «رایحه‌هایی برای بیان شخصیت منحصربه‌فرد شما.»

The positioning and copy are proposals for future customer and brand review. They require legal and claims review before public use. They do not assert measured uniqueness, exclusivity, quality, performance, or customer preference.

## 3. Customer profile and positioning

The initial customer hypothesis is a modern fragrance enthusiast who values personal expression, distinctive fragrance identities, thoughtful design, and clear, credible descriptions. This is not a validated demographic or market segment.

The collection should aim for expressive, contemporary identities that remain understandable and approachable. Customer research is still needed on language, discovery behavior, desirability, price expectations, sampling interest, and the meaning of “premium-leaning” to the intended audience.

## 4. Signature Collection product model

- **Offering:** A future collection of separately identifiable, AROMAN-branded finished fragrances.
- **Creative model:** AROMAN-directed original development based on approved creative briefs and a future qualified professional development/manufacturing process.
- **Partner model:** A qualified external fragrance-development and/or manufacturing partner may be evaluated later; no partner is identified or approved.
- **Rights preference:** Exclusive commercial rights are preferred. Formula ownership or strong licensing and manufacturing-transfer rights should be evaluated with qualified legal and IP advice. No rights are currently established.
- **Initial assortment:** A small focused collection is a planning recommendation, not an approved SKU count or launch decision.
- **Undecided:** Launch assortment, fragrance selection, sizes, concentration, bottle, packaging, formulation, price, sampling, channel and rollout.
- **Boundary with C:** Customer-selectable Controlled Personalization is a distinct prospective product line and is not included in Signature Collection B.
- **Boundary with Studio:** Guided Studio, Creative Studio and Smart Perfumer remain conceptual exploration tools. Their notes, layer assignments and suggestions are not formulas, ingredient lists, production instructions, or evidence of orderability.

Any physical sample, manufacturing activity, document exchange, commercialization, or public availability requires separate authorization and evidence under the applicable product-line gates. No product may be described as exclusive, manufactured, purchasable, safe, approved, or available without the exact supporting rights and qualified evidence.

## 5. Initial creative briefs

All names are working names. These territories describe intended creative impressions only; they do not specify ingredients, proportions, concentrations, chemical compatibility, actual sensory outcomes, safety, performance, or production methods.

### B-01 — Clear Signal

**Working name.**

- **Character:** Fresh, aromatic, mineral.
- **Mood:** Clarity, movement and contemporary confidence.
- **Sensory direction:** Citrus-like brightness, green aromatic freshness, mineral coolness and clean woody impressions.
- **Avoid:** Generic sporty freshness and derivative identity.

### B-02 — Velvet Tension

**Working name.**

- **Character:** Floral, textured and contrasting.
- **Mood:** Elegance, creativity and individuality.
- **Sensory direction:** Translucent florality, subtle powdery softness, dry spice and textured woods.
- **Avoid:** Conventional sweet-floral stereotypes and novelty for its own sake.

### B-03 — Slow Ember

**Working name.**

- **Character:** Warm, woody and atmospheric.
- **Mood:** Intimacy, warmth and restrained confidence.
- **Sensory direction:** Dry woods, subtle spice, resin-like warmth and muted smoky impressions.
- **Avoid:** Excessive sweetness, heaviness and generic warmth.

These are internal creative briefs, not claims that a resulting fragrance exists or will produce the described impression.

## 6. Internal evaluation criteria

If qualified development and sample review are separately authorized in the future, creative evaluation may consider:

- Alignment with the relevant approved brief.
- Perceived distinctiveness and identity.
- Desirability for the intended customer, tested rather than assumed.
- Meaningful distinction among concepts in the collection.
- Clarity and credibility of any customer-facing explanation.
- Fit with AROMAN's visual and emotional identity.

These are subjective creative criteria. They do not replace qualified technical, quality, safety, regulatory, labeling, IP, manufacturing, commercial, or operational review. A sample impression does not by itself establish repeatability, safety, legal approval, or release readiness.

## 7. Open owner decisions

- Target price position and price ceiling.
- Development and launch budget, including approval limits.
- Validation of the intended customer segment and positioning.
- Final assortment size, fragrance selection and release scope.
- Bottle sizes, concentration and packaging strategy.
- Sampling or discovery experience.
- Sales channel and initial launch geography within the intended market.
- Exact exclusivity, formulation ownership, licensing and manufacturing-transfer objectives.
- Responsible product, finance, legal, quality, safety, regulatory and release reviewers.
- Whether B may launch commercially independently of C, whether both launch together, or whether neither proceeds yet.

No answer should be inferred from this brief or from the current demonstration catalog.

## 8. Dependencies and production-readiness restrictions

Before any product development or release decision, the applicable work must establish a specific contracting entity and site, rights, product scope, qualified partner evidence, samples and review criteria, quality and traceability evidence, Iran-specific professional review, labeling and claims responsibility, supply and capacity, packaging, pricing and commercial terms, fulfillment, support and withdrawal processes. Relevant evidence must be recorded and independently reviewed within its proper scope.

The B gates are independent from the C Controlled Personalization gates. Evidence for B does not establish C readiness, and C evidence does not establish B readiness. This brief changes no gate in [M1-F Production Go/No-Go](M1F_PRODUCTION_GO_NO_GO.md) and does not authorize outreach, document requests, negotiation, sampling, purchasing, manufacturing, or customer sales.
