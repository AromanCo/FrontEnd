First record applicable choices in the [owner decision register](M6_A15_OWNER_DECISION_REGISTER.md). Capture timestamp, tested revision, topology/config identifiers (never secret values), test operator role, expected/actual outcome, and redacted evidence for each test. Any unexplained failure is a no-go.

## Release decision rule

Private exploration may be proposed for owner acceptance only when D1/D2 are recorded, every private-blocking row has passed in the approved target topology, all unresolved applicable conditions are closed, and an accountable owner signs the evidence package. CI or local tests may support but cannot replace target tests. Any scope change to accounts, persistent concepts, public access, real user data or commerce invalidates this scope acceptance and requires its separate gates.
