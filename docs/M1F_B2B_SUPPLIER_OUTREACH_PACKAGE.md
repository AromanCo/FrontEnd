# M1-F.B.2-B — Supplier Outreach Preparation Package

**Status:** Draft templates only. No supplier contact is authorized or made by this document.
**Product direction:** Iran is the intended initial market; B and C are approved directions, not verified capabilities or commercial commitments. Launch sequence remains undecided.

This package supports a two-stage process: (1) a short identity/capability screen, then (2) a scoped document request only after a relevant response and separate AROMAN authorization. A message template is **not permission to send it**. Do not add an invented sender, legal entity, address, purchase commitment, volume forecast, price target, or claim of readiness.

## Use and handling rules

- Select only the candidate and product-line scope explicitly approved in the decision tracker.
- Verify the recipient and contracting entity before sharing non-public information.
- Do not send Studio compositions, customer data, formulas, security-sensitive information, or confidential commercial assumptions in the screening stage.
- Do not request confidential formula details in the initial inquiry. Stage-two technical information requires an approved confidentiality approach and secure channel.
- A response, website, certificate copy, or document receipt is a claim/evidence item, not independent verification or approval. Record the source, date, scope, and reviewer in the evidence register.
- Keep B finished-product questions separate from C controlled-option questions. Do not describe arbitrary Studio concepts as formulas or production specifications.
- Use placeholders only after owner approval. Do not send to a guessed address or public contact form.
